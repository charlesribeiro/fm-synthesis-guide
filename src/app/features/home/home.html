An unofficial, educational instrument

Learn six-operator FM synthesis by seeing it, then hearing it.

DX7 Algorithm Lab walks you through the 32 classic operator-routing algorithms one at a time — an interactive diagram for every algorithm, a guided lesson for what it teaches, and live sound so you can hear each change immediately.

How a lesson works

  1. See the routing

    Every algorithm is drawn from data — carriers, modulators, and the one feedback loop.

  2. Hear the sound

    Play a note and listen to what that specific topology produces.

  3. Change one thing

    Adjust a ratio, a level, or the feedback amount, and hear exactly why it changed.
