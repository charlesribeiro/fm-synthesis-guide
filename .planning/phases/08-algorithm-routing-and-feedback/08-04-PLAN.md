regression, and a completed phase validation record.

  <what-built>

  <how-to-verify>
**Setup.** Use headphones, start at a low volume, raise gradually. Run
`npm run start:harness` and open `http://localhost:4200/dev/worklet-harness.html`.
Click "Enable audio" first — nothing plays before that gesture.

**Why four algorithms and not five:** every one of the 32 rows in this dataset
already declares a feedback self-loop, so there is no feedback-free algorithm to
pick as a distinct fifth sample. D-12's "plus feedback at maximum depth" is
therefore covered by re-playing one of the four at feedback level 7 (check 5).

**Check 1 — Additive Stacks (choose any algorithm with id 1 to 6).** Select it,
leave feedback at 0, play. Expect a clear, musical tone. Confirm it is audible at
a comfortable level and not distorted.

**Check 2 — Tree/Branch (any id 7 to 18).** Select and play. Expect a distinctly
different, more complex timbre than check 1. Confirm the character changes when
you switch between the two.

**Check 3 — Rooting (any id 19 to 25).** Select and play. Confirm it sounds
different again and remains clean at feedback 0.

**Check 4 — Parallel (any id 26 to 32).** Select and play. Algorithm 32 in
particular should sound like stacked pure tones rather than a modulated timbre.

**Check 5 — Maximum feedback (D-07, D-08).** Return to any one of the four,
raise the feedback slider to 7, and play. Expect it to sound harsh and edgy —
that is intended and is what the phase teaches. What you are judging is whether
it stays *bounded*: it must not be painfully loud, must not keep getting louder
the longer you hold it, and must not produce silence, clicking or digital
garbage. Then switch to the maximum-operator-level control and repeat. Report the
loudness relative to check 1.

**Check 6 — Held-note algorithm switch (D-13, ROADMAP criterion 3).** Start a
note, and while it is still sounding change the algorithm select. Expect the
sound to change character without the note stopping, restarting or clicking
sharply. Do this at least three times, including at least one switch between two
algorithms whose feedback operator differs. Then stop. Confirm silence — no
stuck, sounding voice.

**Check 7 — The app itself.** In a second terminal run `npm start` and open
`http://localhost:4200/playground`. Enable audio and play the on-screen keyboard.
This is now the routed worklet engine, not the Phase 5 approximation. Confirm the
notes sound correct, are click-free on attack and release, and stop cleanly.
Switch algorithms from the app while holding a note and confirm the same
behaviour as check 6.

**Check 8 — Lesson 6 regression (D-03).** Open
`http://localhost:4200/learn/algorithm-1`. Perform the lesson's stated try-this
action and trigger a note. Confirm the completion state still fires, the lesson's
described sonic change is actually audible against the new engine, and the try-this
guidance still matches what you hear.

**Check 9 — Honesty copy (D-05, AUDIO-03).** Confirm the persistent educational
approximation label still appears and still reads exactly as before. Routing and
feedback becoming real does not license any stronger claim; report any wording
that now overstates what the engine is.
  </how-to-verify>
