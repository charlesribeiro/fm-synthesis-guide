<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: Listen to it — loudness safety, click-free ramps, and the narrow-viewport backstop</name>
  <what-built>
    A monophonic Web Audio approximation engine behind the `SynthEngine` interface, playable from a
    fixed one-octave on-screen and computer keyboard inside Playground, gated behind an explicit
    "Enable audio" gesture, patched generically across all 32 algorithms including feedback, and
    labelled throughout as an educational approximation.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Start the dev server with `npm start` and open the Playground route.

1. **Gesture gate (AUDIO-01).** On first load, confirm no sound and no browser console warning
   about an AudioContext created outside a user gesture. Confirm the keyboard is visibly inert and
   that pressing a computer key or clicking a key does nothing. Click "Enable audio" and confirm
   the gate disappears and the keys become interactive.
2. **Loudness safety (D-03, T-05-02).** With your system volume at a normal listening level,
   play a note. It should be clearly audible but never startling. Then select Algorithm 32 (six
   simultaneous carriers, the loudest case) and play again — confirm it is louder but still
   comfortable and not distorted or clipped. Then raise feedback to its maximum and play the
   algorithms that carry feedback; confirm nothing squeals or runs away. If any of this is
   uncomfortable, say so — the fixed master gain is meant to be adjusted here.
3. **Click-free ramps (A2).** Play staccato notes repeatedly. Listen for a click or pop at the
   start or the end of each note. Then hold one key and press another before releasing the first
   (the D-04 retrigger): it should read as a clean, fresh attack on the new note, not as a legato
   slide and not as a click. If it feels sluggish the ramp is too long; if you hear a click it is
   too short.
4. **Held-note algorithm switch (D-02).** Hold a note and change the selected algorithm. The note
   must keep sounding and the timbre must change immediately and audibly. Release the key and
   confirm silence. Repeat a few times quickly and confirm nothing is left sounding.
5. **Stuck-voice hunt (AUDIO-02).** While holding a note, try each of: alt-tab away from the
   browser, navigate to another route in the app, and drag the pointer off the key. Each must end
   the note.
6. **Narrow-viewport keyboard (UI-SPEC backstop).** Shrink the window (or use device emulation at
   roughly 360px wide). The 12 keys must stay on one row, must not shrink below a comfortable
   thumb target, and must be reachable by scrolling the row horizontally.
7. **Keyboard access and reduced motion.** Tab to a key and play it with Space. Confirm focus is
   clearly visible. Enable your OS reduced-motion setting and confirm the pressed-key treatment is
   still visible and nothing animates.
8. **Approximation honesty (AUDIO-03, Pitfall 7).** Confirm the label "Educational approximation —
   not a bit-accurate DX7 emulation" is visible in every state, including before enabling audio.
   Then read every other piece of copy on the page and flag any wording that implies accuracy,
   authenticity, or emulation without qualification.

Report, for each of items 2 and 3, whether the current values are right or which way they should
move. Report any copy flagged in item 8 verbatim.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" if everything above holds, or describe the issues — including which tuning constants should move and in which direction.</resume-signal>
