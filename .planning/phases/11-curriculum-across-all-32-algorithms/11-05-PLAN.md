<objective>
Turn the completed thirty-two-lesson dataset into the `/learn` index a learner actually meets: four
labelled curriculum sections in D-02's order, each with its own completed-of-total count, plus an
overall count across the whole curriculum — then verify by hand what no test can, and close the phase's
validation record.

This is the phase's only user-facing plan and its only non-autonomous one. The blocking checkpoint
covers the two things the whole phase rests on that automation cannot judge: whether thirty new lesson
explanations are honest, specific teaching prose rather than templated filler, and whether a thirty-two
card index still reads as a curriculum rather than as a wall.

Purpose: close CURR-01's "progress is tracked" clause and the roadmap's "grouped by recurring structure,
not rote memorization" success criterion.
Output: a grouped, counted `/learn` index; a restructured component spec; a completed `11-VALIDATION.md`.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `LessonProgress` in-memory state -> rendered counts | Session-only state read through a facade; nothing crosses a storage or network boundary |
| Lesson prose and group descriptions -> rendered page | Static, compile-time, originally authored content rendered through Angular's interpolation, which escapes by default |

documented rule, and a try-this experiment chosen from the same routing by a second documented rule. The

Parallel, Additive Stacks, Tree and Branch, Rooting, with a completed-of-total count for each section
and one for the whole curriculum.

Every claim that automation can check is already checked: a lesson-to-algorithm bijection, the
curriculum ordering against the dataset's own teaching tags, four non-empty groups, the additive-like
membership set, the duplicate clusters' preset equality, try-this reachability on all thirty-two rows,
prose shape, live count updates, order stability, and heading hierarchy. This checkpoint covers only
what those cannot judge.
  </what-built>

  <how-to-verify>
Run `npm start` and keep the dev server running for the whole check. Work through all six checks and
report findings per check, not just an overall verdict.

**Check 1 — index legibility at thirty-two cards.** Visit `/learn`. Confirm the four sections read as
four distinct bands rather than as one undifferentiated wall of cards; that each section's heading and
one-sentence description make its recurring shape clear before you read any card; and that the overall
and per-section counts are findable at a glance. Try it at a narrow viewport as well as a wide one.

**Check 2 — duplicate-cluster prose honesty.** Read the explanations for the nine lessons in the three
duplicate-topology clusters: Algorithms 13 and 14 (identical to Algorithm 1 and to each other),
Algorithms 3, 4 and 11 (identical to one another), and Algorithms 24, 25 and 31 (identical to one
another). For each, confirm the text acknowledges the repeated shape plainly and does not assert a
routing difference the dataset does not declare. A lesson that reads as specific and confident while
describing a difference that does not exist is the exact failure this check exists to catch.

**Check 3 — distinguishing feature across the rest.** Sample at least eight of the remaining
twenty-one new lessons across all four groups and confirm each names a concrete structural feature of
its own algorithm — chain length, branch count, feedback-operator position, independent-path count —
rather than reading as group boilerplate with a number swapped in. Confirm at least one
feedback-relocated pair reads as genuinely two lessons: suggested pairs are Algorithm 8 against
Algorithm 9, or Algorithm 16 against Algorithm 17.

**Check 4 — the presets are usable teaching starting points.** Open four lessons spanning the four
groups, enable audio, and play each one's starting patch. Confirm each is audible at a comfortable
level, free of clicks, and that its character plausibly matches what its explanation says it will sound
like. Then perform each lesson's try-this step and confirm the described change is actually audible and
that the completion check fires. Suggested spread: Algorithm 31 (Parallel, additive-like), Algorithm 7
(Tree and Branch, deepest chain), Algorithm 22 (Rooting, fan-out) and Algorithm 5 (Additive Stacks,
three pairs).

**Check 5 — keyboard, screen reader, colour and motion.** From `/learn`, using the keyboard alone,
reach and activate a card in the last section, complete that lesson, and return to `/learn`; confirm
visible focus throughout, that section headings are announced in order by a screen reader, and that the
counts are announced when reached. Confirm completion state is legible with colour ignored — the words
Completed and Not started plus the counts should carry it entirely. Confirm nothing animates on
completion with the reduced-motion preference enabled.

**Check 6 — provenance and Algorithm 19.** Confirm no lesson text or preset value came from a
copyrighted patch bank, manual scan, or third-party diagram — everything should be traceable to this
repository's own dataset and rules. Separately, confirm the decision recorded in plan 11-04 for
Algorithm 19: its dataset row carries an `unresolved` provenance flag on its edge list, and this phase
generated its lesson by the same rules as every other row with no learner-facing caveat. Approve that
decision or ask for a different treatment.
  </how-to-verify>
