<objective>
Write eleven lessons: the Additive Stacks group's five remaining algorithms (2 through 6) and the first
half of the Tree and Branch group (7 through 12), all through the structural factory plan 11-01
established.

This is where the machinery meets the dataset's hardest honesty problem. Six of these eleven algorithms
are members of duplicate-topology clusters or feedback-relocated sibling pairs: Algorithms 3, 4 and 11
are pairwise identical; 5 and 6 are identical; 12 is identical to 2; 8 and 9 differ only in which
operator carries the loop; 10 differs from the 3/4/11 cluster only in the same way. D-10 permits family
resemblance and forbids a strained hunt for false distinctions, so these lessons state the relationships
plainly and hang their teaching point on the one thing that genuinely differs.

Purpose: eleven more algorithms covered against CURR-01.
Output: eleven `LESSONS` rows, a `LESSON_IDS` list grown to nineteen, prose-shape invariants applied to
every row, and a cross-check table proving the try-this rule distinguishes the feedback-relocated pairs.
</objective>
