<objective>
Make the per-operator envelope audible where the project actually teaches — the
Algorithm 1 lesson — by giving its carriers a sustained envelope and its
modulators a decaying one, then lock the shipped envelope data behind invariants
and bring the documentation in line with what the engine now does.

Purpose: D-01's whole rationale is that carriers and modulators can now evolve
independently over the life of a note. Until a shipped patch actually differs
between the two roles, that capability exists but is never demonstrated, and the
lesson a learner opens sounds exactly as it did before. This plan is where the
phase's value reaches a user.

Output: an Algorithm 1 lesson whose held note starts bright and mellows, a
mechanical guarantee that no shipped envelope can drone or fail validation, and
documentation that says what shipped without overstating it.
</objective>

**Algorithm 32's starting patch.** Leave every operator on the shared default envelope. Extend that function's existing doc comment with one sentence explaining that Algorithm 32 has no modulation edges at all, so there is no carrier-versus-modulator pair for differentiated envelopes to contrast, and a uniform sustained envelope is the correct expression of that lesson's "six clean, independent partials" point. **Algorithm 1's starting patch.** Give the two operators the canonical dataset derives as carriers the shared default envelope — a fast attack straight to the level maximum, held there, then the default release. Give the four modulator-role operators an envelope that attacks slightly faster, decays through a middle level, and settles at a distinctly lower third-segment level, with the same zero release target. Concretely: rates of eighty, sixteen, sixteen and fifty-five, and levels of ninety-nine, seventy, forty and zero. Under the geometric rate curve, decay rates of sixteen move the modulators from level 99 to 40 in roughly one second; the previously drafted sixties complete that drop in tens of milliseconds and would not be audible as mellowing. That produces a note that opens at full modulation brightness, mellows over roughly a second as the modulators fall to a sustain that is well under half their attack level, and then releases cleanly — the classic FM behaviour the lesson's own explanation already describes in terms of chain depth, now audible over time rather than only across patches.

Do not hardcode which operator ids are carriers. Read the role split from the canonical dataset the same way this file already reads Algorithm 1's edge structure for its output-level comment, so the data and the derivation cannot disagree. Extend the function's doc comment to record the envelope choice and its pedagogical intent alongside the existing output-level rationale, and to name it as the concrete expression of the per-operator envelope capability this phase adds. Keep the frozen-at-every-level and spread-from-the-default construction exactly as it is: nothing here may mutate the shared frozen default objects.

Assert the same invariants over the shared default envelope so the default and the lesson data are held to one standard.
